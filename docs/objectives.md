# Project Objectives — DawnBloom

## 1. Primary Objectives

The primary objective of **DawnBloom — Volunteer & Student Guidance Management System** is to provide an accessible, responsive, and robust frontend information system that connects enthusiastic community volunteers with orphanage students needing educational guidance and life mentorship.

---

## 2. Specific Functional Objectives

1. **Volunteer Engagement & Roster Management:**
   - Enable coordinators to register, update, search, filter, and review volunteer mentors.
   - Maintain records of specific mentor competencies (STEM, literature, arts, computing, career counseling) and availability windows.

2. **Student Guidance Profiles:**
   - Catalog students receiving educational guidance using privacy-preserving display aliases.
   - Map subjects needing reinforcement and assign designated primary volunteer tutors.

3. **Conflict-Aware Scheduling:**
   - Provide conflict validation to prevent duplicate bookings of the same volunteer at identical date and time intervals.
   - Validate that scheduled session and event start times are earlier than designated end times.

4. **Event & Workshop Management:**
   - Schedule group educational workshops, literacy days, and recreational community events.
   - Allow assignment of multiple volunteers to large events and track expected student counts.

5. **Operational Task Tracking:**
   - Enable staff and volunteers to track action items, preparation checklists, and supply procurements.
   - Provide visual completion indicators, status toggles (To Do / In Progress / Completed), and overdue flags.

6. **Impact Reporting & Audit Data Export:**
   - Provide visual SVG/CSS analytical dashboards indicating session completion ratios, subject domain breakdowns, and roster status.
   - Enable coordinators to export complete datasets into standardized CSV files via client-side Blob generation.

---

## 3. Educational & Software Engineering Objectives

1. **Pure Standards-Compliant Web Implementation:**
   - Build a full-featured management portal strictly utilizing HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, and localStorage.
   - Eliminate dependencies on external server frameworks, build pipelines, or third-party database subscriptions.

2. **Aesthetic Excellence & Theme Adherence:**
   - Apply a cohesive color palette (Lavender grey `#868092`, Blush pink `#E2C2D2`, Deep mauve `#4B3D4F`, Dusty rose `#B26C86`, Midnight black `#0C0F17`).
   - Create a calm, welcoming, Pinterest-inspired dark atmospheric UI appropriate for educational showcase.

3. **High Deployment Compatibility:**
   - Ensure complete static hosting compatibility with GitHub Pages using strict relative pathing.
